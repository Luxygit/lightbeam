                     STADGAR FÖR IDEELL FÖRENING: LIGHTBEAM

## § 1. Name and Seat (Namn och säte)
1.1 The name of the non-profit association is **LightBeam** (hereinafter referred to as "the Association").
1.2 The board has its registered office and seat in **[Insert City]**, Sweden.

## § 2. Purpose and Objectives (Ändamål och verksamhet)
2.1 The Association is a non-profit organization (*ideell förening*).
2.2 The primary purpose of the Association is to provide practical, multi-disciplinary internship opportunities for students across various fields of study (including engineering, project management, craftsmanship, and marketing) through hands-on educational projects, such as the restoration and retrofitting of maritime and technical assets.
2.3 The Association is politically and religiously independent.

## § 3. Membership (Medlemskap)
3.1 Membership is open to individuals who actively support the purpose of the Association and are approved by the Board of Directors.
3.2 Members are required to follow these statutes and pay any annual membership fees decided by the Annual General Meeting (AGM).
3.3 Membership is open to any individual or organization supporting the purpose of LightBeam. The board reserves the right to screen and approve active project participants.

## § 4. Student Interns and Participants (Praktikanter och deltagare)
4.1 Students participating in the Association’s internship programs or rotating projects are classified strictly as **"Interns"** (*praktikanter*) or **"Participants"** (*deltagare*).
4.2 Interns are beneficiaries of the Association's educational programs and are not considered formal legal members of the Association.
4.3 Interns do not pay membership fees, do not hold voting rights at the Annual General Meeting (AGM), and cannot be elected to the Board of Directors. 
4.4 Interns have the right to voice opinions and give feedback regarding their projects, but they hold no administrative, financial, or constitutional decision-making power within the Association.

## § 5. The Annual General Meeting - AGM (Årsmöte)
5.1 The Annual General Meeting (AGM) is the highest decision-making body of the Association. It shall be held every year before the end of **[Insert Month]**.
5.2 Notice of the AGM must be sent via email to all formal members at least **two (2) weeks** in advance, accompanied by the agenda.
5.3 Each formal member who has fulfilled their obligations holds **one (1) vote** at the AGM. Voting by proxy is not permitted unless explicitly agreed upon by the board.
5.4 Rotating interns may be invited to attend the AGM as non-voting observers at the discretion of the Board.

## § 6. Agenda of the AGM (Dagordning vid årsmöte)
The ordinary AGM must include the following points:
1. Opening of the meeting.
2. Election of a chairperson and secretary for the meeting.
3. Election of two adjusters to sign the minutes alongside the chairperson.
4. Determination of whether the meeting has been properly convened.
5. Approval of the agenda.
6. Presentation of the Board’s annual report and financial statements for the past fiscal year.
7. Auditor’s report.
8. Vote on granting discharge from liability (*ansvarsfrihet*) to the Board members.
9. Determination of membership fees for the upcoming year.
10. Election of the Chairperson of the Board.
11. Election of other Board members.
12. Election of an Auditor.
13. Election of the Nomination Committee (*valberedning*).
14. Other pre-notified matters.
15. Closing of the meeting.

## § 7. The Board of Directors (Styrelsen)
7.1 The Board of Directors manages the Association’s affairs, operational strategy, budget, and internship intake between AGMs.
7.2 The Board consists of a Chairperson and at least **two (2) other members** (e.g., Treasurer and Secretary).
7.3 The Board is elected at the AGM for a term of one (1) year.
7.4 The Board is quorate when more than half of its members are present. Decisions are made by a simple majority vote. In the event of a tie, the Chairperson holds the casting vote.

## § 8. Firm Signatory (Firmateckning)
8.1 The Association’s name and bank accounts are signed and authorized by the Board of Directors jointly, or individually by person(s) specifically appointed by the Board.

## § 9. Fiscal Year (Räkenskapsår)
9.1 The fiscal year of the Association shall be the calendar year, running from **January 1st to December 31st**.

## § 10. Auditors (Revisorer)
10.1 The Association’s financial accounts and management shall be reviewed annually by an auditor elected at the AGM. The auditor does not need to be a professional certified accountant but must be independent of the Board.

## § 11. Amendment of Statutes (Stadgeändring)
11.1 Amendments to these statutes require a **two-thirds (2/3) majority vote** at an ordinary AGM, or a simple majority at two consecutive general meetings (of which at least one must be an ordinary AGM).

## § 12. Dissolution of the Association (Upplösning)
12.1 Dissolution of the Association requires all members votes consensus at two consecutive general meetings, of which at least one must be an ordinary AGM.
12.2 In the event of dissolution, any remaining assets, property (including boat hulls or tools), and funds must be donated to another non-profit organization or educational institution promoting similar charitable purposes, as decided by the final AGM. Under no circumstances may assets be distributed among members or founders.
