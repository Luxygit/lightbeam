1. The Chairperson (Ordförande)
- The DirectorThe Chairperson ensures the NGO stays true to its purpose and leads the team.
- Leads Meetings: Calls all official meetings to order, guides the discussions, and makes sure everyone gets a say before voting.
- Manages the Big Picture: Checks in on the student internship programs and tracks the progress of the boat hull restoration.
- Official Representative: Acts as the main contact person for outside partners, shipyard owners, and university coordinators.
- Legal Signatory: Steps in to sign official contracts, lease agreements, or donation documents alongside the Treasurer.
2. The Secretary (Sekreterare)
  — The CoordinatorThe Secretary is the backbone of the NGO’s paperwork, organization, and history.
  Writes Meeting Notes: Takes notes at every meeting to record exactly what was decided, who is on the board, and how money is spent.
  Manages the Roster: Keeps an up-to-date, secure list of your core members and a separate schedule tracking the rotating student interns.
  Handles Mail: Monitors the NGO's official email address and sends out meeting invitations to the core team in advance.
  Safeguards Files: Keeps the physical and digital folders of your signed statutes, minutes, and tax papers safe.
  3. The Treasurer (Kassör)
  — The Financial OfficerThe Treasurer manages the NGO's funds and ensures absolute transparency with money.
  Handles the Bank Account: Sets up LightBeam's corporate bank account, deposits donations, and pays invoices for tools or boat parts.
  Tracks the Budget: Keeps a simple spreadsheet logging every single krona (SEK) coming in and going out.
  Values Donated Assets: Records the estimated fair value of the donated boat hull in the books so the NGO's asset registry is accurate.
  Tax Compliance: Submits necessary year-end financial paperwork to the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket) so the NGO keeps its tax-free status.
