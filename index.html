🔎 Project Overview

SunBeam is a permanent, non-profit maritime educational initiative designed to bridge the gap between digital innovation and real-world environmental sustainability. Founded by an IT student from 42 Barcelona holding an active engine boat permit, the project scales modern technology down to an eco-friendly mobile marine platform.

To guarantee absolute fiscal transparency and structural integrity, the vessel will be constructed and renovated entirely by the founder. Once completed, the vessel will transition into a registered European NGO asset, flying a lifetime Polish registration flag (REJA24 System) for unrestricted navigation through the Mediterranean and European inland canals.

🗒️ The 4-Student Crew Configuration

SunBeam will operate on a rolling schedule, hosting exactly four carefully screened European students at any given time. Each multi-month voyage will maintain a balanced team covering four critical technical pillars:

💻 IT Specialist

Manages hardware integrations, monitors system safety protocols, and updates open-source routing algorithms.

⚡ Electrician / Maker

Monitors LiFePO4 battery banks, validates marine wiring, and handles preventative plywood maintenance.

🧪 Marine Biologist

Deploys sampling rigs, tracks regional microplastic metrics, and logs continuous water quality indices.

📈 Growth & Marketing

Documents field progress, updates global backers, and handles digital content production loops.
