🔎 Project Overview

SunBeam is a permanent, non-profit maritime educational initiative designed to bridge the gap between digital innovation and real-world environmental sustainability. Founded by an IT student from 42 School Barcelona holding an engine boat permit and plywood hull repairement expertise, the project scales modern technology down to an eco-friendly mobile marine platform.

To guarantee absolute fiscal transparency and structural integrity, the vessel will be constructed and renovated entirely by the founder. Once completed, the vessel will transition into a registered European NGO asset, flying a lifetime EU registration flag (REJA24 System) for unrestricted navigation through the Mediterranean and European inland canals.

🗒️ The 4-Student Crew Configuration

SunBeam will operate on a rolling schedule, hosting exactly four carefully screened European students at any given time FOR FREE!. Crew members will be selected based on their profile and on-board vision. There are some mandatory conditions detailed in our CREW MEMBER GUIDE Each voyage will maintain a balanced team covering four critical technical pillars:

💻 IT Specialist

We will be hosting one 42 School student, giving them the practical enviromment for them to test and use their IT skill in real life.

⚡ Electrician / Handyman

We would also like to have on board someone more into the manual crafts, be it an electrician interested in solar energy, or a woodcrafter willing to learn about boat hull maintenance.

🧪 Maritime Specialist

Given the nature of our lab, we think it would be a waste not to have someone with some sort of maritime knowledge.

📈 Marketing Intern

We will need to include someone to help us get more widely known since we rely on ongoing private donations.

SunBeam utilizes a purely community-backed, altruistic financial framework. Our upcoming crowdfunding initiative targets a fixed €18,000 baseline budget, with 100% of the funds allocated straight to raw hardware and marine construction components.

Direct Resource Allocation:

  • Hull Refitting & Plywood Marine Structural Adjustments: €7,000

Donors have structural flexibility to contribute any custom capital amount between €20 and €5,000. All contributions serve exclusively to secure the structural lifecycle of the vessel, funding tools that launch the professional trajectories of hundreds of young scientists and engineers across the European continent.
