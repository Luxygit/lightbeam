                     STADGAR FÖR IDEELL FÖRENING: LIGHTBEAM

§1. NAME AND PURPOSE
The name of the non-profit association is LightBeam. The purpose of the 
association is to support European student technical residencies, marine 
research, and digital innovation by operating a mobile, zero-emission 
solar-powered vessel across European waters.

§2. SEAT OF THE ASSOCIATION
The board of the association is headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden.

§3. MEMBERSHIP
Membership is open to any individual or organization supporting the 
purpose of LightBeam. The board reserves the right to screen and approve 
active project participants.

§7. DISSOLUTION OF THE ASSOCIATION

or youth technological education in Europe.
